(`boundary.ts:52`), and `collectDefaultExclusionRows` requires `!node.pageShell && (ownsDirectText ||
structuralBoundary)` (`content/marking/engine.ts:99-107`), **a full-width `<footer>`/`<header>`/`<nav>` that does
not own direct text — i.e. the normal case, a container of links and lists — gets no default exclusion row at
all**, and its text-bearing descendants are then classified `implicit-include` (`domain/evaluate.ts:62-64`) and
**submitted to the AI as content**. The same happens to any toggleable default containing a nested landmark
(`landmarkCount >= 2` → `pageShell` field true → skipped outright) and to any shallow generic node
(`depthFromBody <= 2`, `boundary.ts:44`). Contract `C-MARK-6` ("automatic toggleable-default collection follows
the taxonomy tag UNCONDITIONALLY, subject only to hidden-subtree / immutable-ancestor / consent / extension-UI
skips") is violated.

**Finding B (performance, HIGH — see W-41/W-42).**
the marking engine rebuilds a **full-document DOM bridge whose per-node cost is O(subtree)**, i.e. **O(n²) over
the document**, and it is wired to `scroll`, `resize`, MutationObserver, ResizeObserver and IntersectionObserver
(`engine.ts:201-239` → `:184-200` → `:166-169` → `dom-view.ts:219-230`). The popup then pushes a content
directive **unconditionally every 500 ms** (`main.tsx:829`), and the element that renders it is **not tagged as
extension UI** (`content-loader.content.ts:303-309` vs the mutation filter at `engine.ts:204-207`), so each push
that renders a banner or curtain re-triggers that O(n²) rebuild. This is legacy's CPU-peg class (P14/P16,
`KN:546,548`) reintroduced with a worse asymptote and none of legacy's CP7a caches.

## 0. Headline verdict

**No. The rewrite has not solved every weakness — but the split is sharp and legible.**

| Band | Count | Character |
|---|---:|---|
| SOLVED-BY-DESIGN | 19 | The failure is structurally unreachable — the mechanism that produced it does not exist |
| SOLVED-IN-CODE | 8 | Handled correctly today by convention; a future edit could regress it |
| PARTIAL | 13 | Materially improved, residue remains |
| UNSOLVED | 8 | Present, or reintroduced in a new host |
| NOT-YET-APPLICABLE | 1 | Subsystem half-built; cannot be judged, must be watched |

Those counts cover `W-01…W-49` only. The pain register adds one further regression of its own severity class:
**P10 is unsolved and its blast radius has grown** (see N-0) — the page-shell rule the architect deleted in July
is back, and the rewrite additionally wired it into default-exclusion collection, so footer/header/nav
boilerplate now reaches the AI as included content on most real pages.

The clean win is **vertical**: the marking model's *precedence* semantics, the wire schema, the transport, the
storage authority rule and the render-mode flow are genuinely re-engineered, and about a third of the legacy
catalogue is now impossible rather than merely fixed. The unresolved band is almost entirely **horizontal and
concentrated in two files** —
`src/entrypoints/popup/main.tsx` (1,799 lines, 43 module-level `let`s, 10 hand-written binding-key staleness
guards) and the marking engine's refresh path. Every UNSOLVED item below lives in one of those two places, plus
one process item (no destructive-save guard) and one product decision (`/save` semantics).

Put bluntly: **the rewrite designed out the bug classes legacy's *data model* produced, and re-created the bug
classes legacy's *coordination layer* produced, in a smaller and better-commented form.**

## 5. Answer in one paragraph

The rewrite has solved, structurally and convincingly, the weaknesses that came from legacy's **data model and
transport**: one typed bus with an enumerable route table, one pure domain spine whose single pass produces both
the overlay and the wire payload, one backend-authority rule in one function, one merge (none), one storage area,
one page-world program, no fingerprints, no epochs on marking data, no byte-locked twins, and a render-mode flow
whose entire saga has been deleted rather than repaired. That is roughly 19 items solved by design and another 8
by disciplined implementation. It has **not** solved the weaknesses that came from legacy's **coordination
layer** — those have migrated into `src/entrypoints/popup/main.tsx`, which now polls everything at 2 Hz, decides,
dictates the content organ's surface, keeps a shadow FSM, probes content and writes its conclusion back, and
guards its own interleaving with ten hand-written staleness checks. It has additionally **regressed** three
things legacy had already fixed at real cost — the page-shell rejection rule the architect deleted in July, now
also suppressing default exclusion so footer boilerplate is submitted as content (N-0); the full-document
rescan-on-observer CPU pathology (now O(n²) and driven by a 2 Hz directive push whose rendering root is not
filtered out of its own mutation observer); and the destructive-save class (now deterministic rather than
intermittent, since the payload contains exactly one page by construction) — and introduced three new failure
modes legacy never had: static-mode capture that cannot succeed, a
signal cursor that silently starves after a worker restart, and a property lock whose heartbeat stops when the
panel closes. None of the unresolved items is architectural bedrock; all of them are addressable inside the seams
the rewrite already built, and the machinery for most of the fixes (`decide.ts`, `evaluateBranch`,
`rehydrateDurableFacts`, `publishDirectiveIfChanged`, `project()`, the organ cursor) is already written and
tested — merely unexercised.
